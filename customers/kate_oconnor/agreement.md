# Development Agreement — Voice Coaching App

**Between:** John Davenport *(the "Developer")*
**And:** Kate O'Connor *(the "Client")*
**Date:** June 2, 2026

This is a plain-English agreement so we both know what we're building, what it
costs, and how it gets paid. If anything here doesn't match your understanding,
tell me and we'll fix it before either of us signs.

---

## 1. What we're building

A custom **voice-enabled AI coaching app** — a web app your clients can talk to,
built on your existing coaching material. Think of it as your custom GPT, rebuilt
as your own software that you own and control.

For this first version (the "MVP"), that means:

- **Voice chat** — clients speak to the app and it speaks back.
- **Your knowledge** — your coaching content (~30 pages of markdown) loaded into
  the app so it responds the way you've trained it to.
- **Runs on Claude, not locked to it** — built provider-agnostic, so we can swap
  the underlying AI provider (Claude, or another) at any time without a rewrite.
- **Personal intake** — the app asks each client their name, gender, and pronouns
  so it speaks to everyone appropriately (not women-only).
- **Simple login** — a private login page on your website (or a subdomain). No App
  Store — a regular web app.
- **Subscription billing** — clients subscribe and pay through the app (a simple
  plan), so you get paid directly with no App Store cut.
- **Admin UI** — a basic dashboard where you manage your content and see your
  clients and subscriptions, without needing me for day-to-day.
- **Deployed and live** — hosted and running on your domain, ready for clients.

## 2. What's not included (so there are no surprises)

- A native iPhone/Android app or App Store listing.
- **Advanced** billing (multiple subscription tiers, free trials, coupons,
  failed-payment recovery), a detailed analytics dashboard, or full client
  conversation history. The MVP includes a *simple* subscription and a *basic*
  admin UI; anything beyond that I'd quote separately if you want it later.
- Writing or editing your coaching content — you provide that material.
- Ongoing **running costs** (see Section 6).
- Ongoing or long-term **support/maintenance** (see Section 7).

## 3. What I need from you

- Your coaching content.
- Access to your domain/hosting, or permission to set it up for you.
- Timely answers as questions come up.

The clock on delivery starts once I have your content and the deposit.

that scope, I'll quote it separately before doing any work — you'll never get a
surprise charge.

## 6. Ongoing running costs

The price above is to **build** the app. Once it's live, an AI app has running
costs — the AI provider's usage fees (Claude or whoever we're on), voice/speech
processing, hosting, and your domain. **Those are yours**, and wherever possible
they'll be billed straight to your own accounts so you see and control them. I'll
set everything up and show you what to expect before we go live.

## 7. Getting it working — and the limits of support

I want the app to work and I'll make sure it does: we'll go through a couple of
rounds of revisions to get it right, and I'll fix genuine bugs that turn up in the
first **30 days** after launch so it does what we agreed.

Beyond that, **this agreement does not include ongoing or long-term support or
maintenance.** At this price there simply isn't room for it. In practice I'll
often help out informally and keep an eye on things as a courtesy — but that's
goodwill, not a contractual commitment, so please don't rely on it for anything
critical.

If you want guaranteed ongoing support and maintenance, we can set that up as a
separate paid arrangement.

## 8. Who owns what

When the build is paid in full, **you own the app and its code** and the coaching
content you provided. I keep the right to reuse the general tools, libraries, and
techniques I used to build it (the provider-agnostic framework, etc.) — I'm not
handing you exclusive rights to my toolkit, just to your finished product.

## 9. Changes and revisions

Small tweaks and reasonable revisions within the agreed scope are included. Bigger
additions get their own quote first. Either of us can pause or walk away with
written notice; if we stop partway, you pay for the work done to that point and
keep what's been built.

## 10. Plain-English catch-all

We're both acting in good faith. If something comes up that this doesn't cover, 
we'll talk it through and write down what we decided.
